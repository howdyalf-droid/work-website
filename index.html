Where strategy meets identity

As creative director at two design-led branding agencies, my role was to shape the strategy, then lead the team in bringing it to life. Branding isn't decoration — it's the articulation of what an organisation believes and why that matters to the people it wants to reach.

Recent clients include Development Victoria, Parks Victoria, Cornwalls Lawyers, AustralianSuper, and a range of property developments, professional services firms, and not-for-profits.

The thinking behind the work

I'm a grazer. I pick up interesting thinking from here and there — gathering stories, fables and oddities, watching how people behave, their foibles and prejudices. I look for insightful connections, links that lead to creative ways to solve problems.

That instinct has shaped how I approach strategy: less framework, more forensic curiosity. Whether I'm working on a brand platform, a campaign brief or a content strategy, I'm looking for the one true thing that makes an idea worth having.

Copy-based. Strategically minded.

I started as a copywriter and have never really stopped thinking like one. Over thirty years I've worked on brands including Mercedes-Benz, Holden, RACV, TAC, Ford, Jeep, Bonduelle, headspace, Bupa and Movember — across agencies including Saatchi & Saatchi, Clemenger BBDO, Grey and McCann.

The best advertising I've done has always started with a genuine understanding of the audience. Everything else — the craft, the format, the channel — follows from that.
